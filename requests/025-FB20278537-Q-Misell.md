## Descriptive Title

Push-notification Interoperability

## Request Type

Interoperability Request

## Feature Requested

Push Notification Service

## Reason for Request

Presently, the only method to receive push notifications on an iOS or iPadOS device is via Apple’s own Push Notification Service.

I am therefore requesting the ability for third-party applications to provide an alternative push notification service on iOS devices not relying on Apple’s APNS.

## Product Description

[UnifiedPush](https://unifiedpush.org)

## How the Feature Will Be Used

Provide an alternative means for users to receive push notifications on their Apple devices.

## Where Offered

European Union

## Evaluation of Alternatives

Yes, there appears to be no other method to achieve this.

## Confidentiality

Fully available to other developers

## Communications

##### September 19, 2025 — Apple to Developer

Thank you for your interoperability request. It is currently in Phase I — Eligibility review. We will reach out via this forum if we need clarification of your request. If we do not reach out for clarification, we will endeavor to provide you with the outcome of the initial assessment of our Phase I — Eligibility review within 20 working days of the date of your request, which does not include any public holiday as defined by the European Commission.

##### October 15, 2025 — Apple to Developer

We have reviewed your submission and determined that your request—seeking the ability for an app to establish a persistent or periodic connection with a server—is not a feature accessed by Apple services or hardware that lacks interoperability. In your example (i.e., iOS push notifications), the ability to establish a persistent or periodic connection with an external server is part of the operating system, rather than an Apple service. The registration of the device with the push notification server is an operating system feature.

Moreover, Apple already offers a mechanism—Apple Push Notification Service—to initiate push notifications to Apple operating systems, and that mechanism is available to both Apple and third parties.

For these reasons, we are not moving your request to the next phase of our interoperability review process.

This concludes our DMA Article 6(7) review.
