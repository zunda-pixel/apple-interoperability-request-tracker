## Descriptive Title

Ensure that ManagedSettingsUI.ShieldConfiguration and ManagedSettings.ShieldActionDelegate gains same level of functionality as Apple’s built-in Screen Time functionality

## Request Type

Interoperability Request

## Feature Requested

Screen Time

## Reason for Request

My name is Frederik Riedel and I’m the developer behind an app called “one sec.” With one sec, we help millions of users around the world to save more than 1000+ years from scrolling social media every single week. We help grown-up adult users to limit their own screen time (not adolescents who are managed by their parents).

For this, we make use of ManagedSettingsUI.ShieldConfiguration in order to display block shield overlays on top of target apps (e.g. when a user has reached a certain limit or during a block session). We use ManagedSettings.ShieldActionDelegate in order to react to user actions on block shields.

We have noticed, however, that Apple’s capabilities in their own native Screen Time solution go far beyond what is available to 3rd party developers:

* Apple’s shields can display animations (the hourglass rotates nicely when the shield appears or disappears). The 3rd party ManagedSettingsUI.ShieldConfiguration cannot.
* Apple can display overlays and additional custom UI elements on top of the Screen Time shield — for example, when pressing the “Ignore Limit For Today” button. This option is not available for 3rd party developers, neither through ManagedSettingsUI.ShieldConfiguration nor through ManagedSettings.ShieldActionDelegate. It would be great if Apple allowed 3rd party developers to display UIViewControllers on top of the Screen Time shield as well.

## Product Description

With one sec, we help millions of users around the world to save more than 1000+ years from scrolling social media every single week. We help grown-up adult users to limit their own screen time (not adolescents who are managed by their parents).

## How the Feature Will Be Used

We would heavily improve our Screen Time shields (ManagedSettingsUI.ShieldConfiguration and ManagedSettings.ShieldActionDelegate):

* They would be able to offer nicer animations (e.g. when a shield appears or disappears).
* We would also be able to offer our interventions (e.g. a full-screen breathing exercise) within the Screen Time shield without the need to forward the user into our app first. This would be possible if Apple gave us access to use UIKit or SwiftUI within the shield, just how they utilize it for their own Screen Time solution.

## Where Offered

Worldwide

## Evaluation of Alternatives

No other Screen Time frameworks are available on iOS.

## Confidentiality

Fully available to other developers

## Communications

##### July 14, 2025 — Apple to Developer

Thank you for your interoperability request. It is currently in Phase I — Eligibility review. We will reach out via this forum if we need clarification of your request. If we do not reach out for clarification, we will endeavor to provide you with the outcome of the initial assessment of our Phase I — Eligibility review within 20 working days of the date of your request, which does not include any public holiday as defined by the European Commission.

##### August 11, 2025 — Apple to Developer

Thank you for your request. Following an initial assessment of your request, we are moving your request to the next phase of our interoperability process. In this phase, the relevant teams will assess whether we are able to provide an effective interoperability solution and, if so, develop a tentative project plan.

It may be that, despite our best efforts, it will ultimately not be feasible or appropriate to provide an interoperability solution, including if providing effective interoperability would compromise the integrity of the operating system. We have also not yet conducted an assessment of the impact this request would have on Apple’s intellectual property rights. We will inform you should it not be feasible or appropriate to implement your request. We expect to have an update for you on this phase by October 7.

##### October 7, 2025 — Apple to Developer

Thank you for your request. First, we note that iOS 26 removed animation from the Screen Time shield. Accordingly, there is no longer a gap in capabilities to address between Screen Time and third-party apps as far as shield animation.

We are still, however, moving the shield button portion of your request to Phase III (Development) of our interoperability process. We plan to provide an optional menu with up to 3 customizable menu buttons launched from the secondary shield button, similar to the three menu button options that Screen Time offers to users today. This is a mild engineering effort. We plan to complete development of this solution by March 2026, and ship it shortly thereafter. We will update you once we have shipped it in a beta release.

We believe this addresses your request. Please let us know within 5 working days if you have any feedback on the proposed plan. If we do not hear from you in that time frame, we will begin development work in accordance with the plan described above.

##### October 8, 2025 — Developer to Apple

Thanks a lot for the update! First of all, I’m a bit sad to hear that the animations from the Screen Time shields have been removed. The goal of my feedback request was definitely not to take away joy from Apple’s default Screen Time shields. It would be great if long-term you could revise this decision and find a solution how all developers can make use of animations in the shield.

But I agree, for now let’s focus on the shield button options — it sounds great that this functionality will be made available to 3rd party developers as well. To be able to provide useful feedback, it would be good to get some more insights about the implementation details of this new functionality:

* How will the new menu button options be triggered to be displayed within the shield? I assume it might be a new return enum in the ShieldActionDelegate, something like ShieldActionResponse.moreOptions(button1, button2, button3)?
* How will the titles and actions of the button options be configured?
* Will .openParentApp be added to the feasible enums of ShieldActionResponse as well?

##### October 15, 2025 — Apple to Developer

The team is continuing to do the detailed scoping necessary for implementation. In response to your first two questions, the project plan is that the ShieldConfiguration API will take a new optional string array (limited to max 3 sub menu label strings). As per your third question, the plan does not include the addition of .openParentApp.

##### October 16, 2025 — Developer to Apple

Just to avoid any misunderstandings, I assume there will also be a way to respond and execute code reacting to a user interaction when the user selects one of the three new buttons?

##### November 3, 2025 — Apple to Developer

Yes, the current plan is to extend the existing ShieldAction enumeration with new cases for each submenu button. Developers will be able to execute code specific to whichever option was selected by the user.

##### April 29, 2026 — Apple to Developer

In March, Apple shipped the solution to your interoperability request. The solution enhanced the Screen Time shield customization options with new parameters.

For more information on the solution we shipped, please refer to the following developer documentation:

* <https://developer.apple.com/documentation/managedsettingsui/shieldconfiguration/secondarybuttonsubmenuitems>
* <https://developer.apple.com/documentation/managedsettingsui/shieldconfiguration/secondarybuttonlabel>

This concludes our work on your interoperability request, and we will close your request out as complete.

If you have any questions on the solution we shipped, please reach out to us through Apple Developer Forums, Feedback Assistant, or a code-level support request.
